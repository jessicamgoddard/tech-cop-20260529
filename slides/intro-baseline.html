What is Baseline?

Baseline is an indicator of whether a feature is safe to use across all major browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

MDN marks every CSS property page with its current Baseline status. CanIUse.com is also a great resource.

All slides in this deck include Baseline features, either Newly or Widely Available.

✓ Widely available

In all major browsers for 30+ months. Safe for any project.

✓ Newly available

Just landed in all major browsers. Usable today, but consider your support window.

⚠ Limited availability

Not yet supported in all major browsers. Use with caution or a fallback.
